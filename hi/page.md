# The page a holder reads

## Intent

For somebody holding an NFT and wondering whether a drop is coming, the page is Rain; the contract, the client and the keeper are implementation they should never meet. It answers two questions in the row itself — am I in, and what am I owed — and it answers them before anybody connects a wallet. Its hardest rule is about hope: it may only say a drop is coming when there is evidence one is, and must say waiting, and why, the rest of the time. A word that turns out to mean nothing is worse than no word.

## Criteria

- **PAGE-1**  Somebody can read every rain on the hub, what each pays and when, with no wallet connected.
- **PAGE-2**  A rain tells whoever is reading it whether they are in it.
- **PAGE-3**  A link to a single rain opens that rain, however cold the visit and wherever it was shared.
- **PAGE-4**  Opening a rain ends on the rain that was just opened, not back on the form.
- **PAGE-5**  The page never says a drop is coming without evidence that something is coming.
  - **PAGE-5.a**  A rain nothing appears to be servicing reads as waiting on a schedule that cannot be read right now.
  - **PAGE-5.b**  A rain that cannot pay says why in the reader's own words — no tickets yet, nobody checked in, an empty pot, or a drop too small to split.
- **PAGE-6**  Nothing about keepers, upkeeps or escrow reaches somebody who came for a pot and a ticket.
- **PAGE-7**  A pot reads in the units a person thinks in rather than in raw base units.
  - **PAGE-7.a**  A pot in a token whose scale is not known yet is never printed as whole tokens.
- **PAGE-8**  Somebody who sent something gets a receipt with a link they can check somewhere we do not control.
- **PAGE-9**  The hub's own id is a link out to a block explorer, because checking it elsewhere is the one thing the page cannot fake.
- **PAGE-10**  No link can aim the page at a different contract.
- **PAGE-11**  The page works at phone width with nothing running off the side.
- **PAGE-12**  Every control stays readable in both themes, including the ones that are switched off.
- **PAGE-13**  Somebody driving the page from a keyboard or a screen reader lands in the right place when it changes.
- **PAGE-14**  A rain id that does not exist says so, rather than sitting on a screen that looks like it is still loading.
- **PAGE-15**  A chain with no hub on it says Rain lives elsewhere rather than rendering an empty hub.
- **PAGE-16**  Somebody can connect any of the common Algorand wallets without configuring anything.
- **PAGE-17**  Every action a person takes is one approval in their wallet.
- **PAGE-18**  Nothing that spends money is clickable while the page is unsure it is talking to the right chain.
- **PAGE-19**  A send that fails says what the chain said, on the page.
- **PAGE-20**  A rain says when its next drop is due in time a person can plan around rather than in rounds.
