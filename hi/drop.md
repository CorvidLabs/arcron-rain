# How a drop falls

## Intent

A drop is a slice of the pot, never the pot. That single decision is what lets the same rain fall every day and lets somebody fill it once and stop thinking about it. Three shapes cover what people actually mean by rain — everyone who entered, whoever showed up, or one lucky holder — and each should behave the way its name implies without anybody reading a specification. When a drop cannot be made fairly it should not be made at all, and nothing should be taken from the pot for it.

## Criteria

- **DROP-1**  Each drop takes only the drip out of the pot, so the same rain can fall again tomorrow.
- **DROP-2**  A rain that falls on everyone gives every ticket the same share.
- **DROP-3**  Somebody who entered late collects the drops that fall after them and none of the ones before.
- **DROP-4**  A rain that falls on everyone costs the same to fall whether ten people or ten thousand are in it.
- **DROP-5**  A rain that falls on whoever showed up pays only the people who checked in during that interval.
- **DROP-6**  Whoever opens a rain that falls on whoever showed up decides how many people one drop is split between.
- **DROP-7**  Seats in a drop go to whoever checked in first.
- **DROP-8**  A share nobody came back for returns to the pot at the next drop rather than being lost.
- **DROP-9**  A drop too small to give everybody in it something is skipped without taking anything out of the pot.
- **DROP-10**  A drop the pot cannot cover is skipped without taking anything out of the pot.
- **DROP-11**  Nobody has to be awake at the moment a rain falls to get their share.
- **DROP-12**  A rain says roughly how many more drops its pot has left in it.
- **DROP-13**  A rain that is declining every drop reads as stopped rather than going on advertising itself as due.
