# Drops happen without anybody tending them

## Intent

Nothing on a chain wakes itself, so something has to poke the hub — and the entire point of Rain is that neither the person filling a pot nor the people in it should ever have to care who. A keeper network does the poking for a fee escrowed by whoever runs the hub, and the rain itself decides whether anything falls, so a stranger poking early changes nothing. A quiet week should look uneventful rather than broken, because a schedule that reports failure when there is simply nothing to do is a schedule that gets switched off. What the cadence really promises should be stated as honestly as the cadence itself.

## Criteria

- **SCHEDULE-1**  A filled rain keeps dripping with nobody tending it.
- **SCHEDULE-2**  Somebody hoping to catch a drop never has to know what wakes the hub, or that anything does.
- **SCHEDULE-3**  Whoever runs a hub points one schedule at it, once, and that schedule serves every rain on it.
- **SCHEDULE-4**  The schedule is paid for out of its own escrow, never out of anybody's pot.
- **SCHEDULE-5**  Whoever runs a hub can see the escrow behind the schedule running low before the drops stop.
- **SCHEDULE-6**  A rain refuses to fall before its own interval has passed, however often anybody pokes the hub.
- **SCHEDULE-7**  Whoever pokes the hub cannot choose which rain falls or who it pays.
- **SCHEDULE-8**  Anyone at all can poke the hub, so no single account going quiet stops the rains.
- **SCHEDULE-9**  A poke that finds nothing to do does nothing rather than failing.
- **SCHEDULE-10**  A hub holding more rains than one poke can visit serves them in turn, so none waits forever.
- **SCHEDULE-11**  Somebody filling a rain picks its cadence in human words rather than in rounds.
- **SCHEDULE-12**  Somebody told a rain falls daily is told that is an estimate counted in rounds rather than a clock.
- **SCHEDULE-13**  A drop that was missed is skipped rather than replayed as a burst of catch-up drops.
- **SCHEDULE-14**  Somebody opening a rain on a crowded hub is told that every extra rain on it lengthens the wait between their own drops.
- **SCHEDULE-15**  The keeper network Rain leans on never has to know that Rain exists.
