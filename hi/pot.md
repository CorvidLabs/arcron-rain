# Opening a rain and filling it

## Intent

Rain begins with somebody who has a token and wants it to reach people on a schedule. Opening a pot should take one sitting and nobody's permission: name it, say who may enter, what falls, how much and how often, and put the first money in with the same signature. What it costs should be visible before it is paid, and what can never be changed afterwards should be just as visible. After that the only job left is keeping the pot deep enough, which is a choice rather than a chore.

## Criteria

- **POT-1**  Anyone can open a rain on the hub without asking permission from whoever deployed it.
- **POT-2**  Somebody opening a rain settles in one pass who may enter, what it pays, how much falls each time, how often, and which of the three ways it falls.
- **POT-3**  Somebody opening a rain gives it a name that readers see instead of a number.
- **POT-4**  Somebody opening a rain puts the first money in with the same signature that opens it, so it is never open and empty.
  - **POT-4.a**  A rain that pays somebody's own token is filled in the step straight after it opens rather than as a separate errand.
  - **POT-4.b**  Somebody opening a rain that cannot be filled in one signature is told so before they sign, rather than left with a pot that quietly declines every drop.
- **POT-5**  Whoever opened a rain can change how much falls each time.
- **POT-6**  Whoever opened a rain can change how often it falls.
- **POT-7**  Nobody but whoever opened a rain can retune it.
- **POT-8**  Who a rain falls on, what it pays in, and how it falls are settled when it opens and never move again.
- **POT-9**  Anyone can top up somebody else's pot.
  - **POT-9.a**  A top-up in the wrong kind of money is refused rather than swallowed.
- **POT-10**  Somebody opening a rain sees what it costs before they pay for it.
- **POT-11**  The hub takes no cut of a pot.
- **POT-12**  Somebody whose rain has taken on more people than the drip can divide is told that raising the drip is the cure.
- **POT-13**  Somebody opening a rain is told plainly that it can never be closed, deleted, or taken back.
  - **POT-13.a**  Somebody putting money into a pot is told there is no path back out of it.
- **POT-14**  Somebody opening a rain that pays their own token has the hub made ready to hold that token as part of opening.
- **POT-15**  Somebody who picks a drip or a cadence the hub will not honour is turned back before they pay for the rain.
- **POT-16**  Money in one rain's pot is only ever spent on that rain's drops.
