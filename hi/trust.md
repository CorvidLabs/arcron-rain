# What the hub promises and what it refuses

## Intent

A pot anybody can put money into is only worth using if the rules cannot change under it, so a hub has no owner, no update path, no pause and no rake — and the same sentence that makes that a guarantee makes it a hazard, because nobody can fix a bug in it either. Both halves get said, in front of the person deciding, not in a footnote afterwards. The remaining trust has to be checkable by somebody who does not believe us: the running code is provably the published code, and the things Rain does not enforce are listed as plainly as the things it does.

## Criteria

- **TRUST-1**  Nobody can change the rules of a running hub, including whoever deployed it.
  - **TRUST-1.a**  Somebody who does not take that on faith can see for themselves that the hub declares no way to update or delete itself.
- **TRUST-2**  Whoever deployed a hub has no more authority on it than a stranger.
- **TRUST-3**  Nobody can pause a rain, seize a pot, or redirect a drop.
- **TRUST-4**  Somebody who does not trust us can check for themselves that the running hub is the code published here.
- **TRUST-5**  Somebody about to put money in is told first that no third party has reviewed the hub.
- **TRUST-6**  Somebody about to put money in is told first that a bug in the hub can never be patched.
- **TRUST-7**  The hub refuses to pay in a token whose issuer could freeze it or claw it back out from under the people who entered for it.
- **TRUST-8**  A payment the hub accepts can never carry a rekey or a close-out that empties the account that sent it.
- **TRUST-9**  Nobody can spend somebody else's payment to buy themselves a ticket or a rain.
- **TRUST-10**  What the hub cannot do is written as plainly as what it can, where somebody deciding whether to use it will find it.
- **TRUST-11**  A hub nobody should use any more says so where somebody would go looking for it.
- **TRUST-12**  Somebody told a hub is superseded is also told what is wrong with it.
- **TRUST-13**  Somebody holding a ticket on a hub being left behind is warned before it happens that their ticket and its cost stay there.
- **TRUST-14**  A hub nobody should use any more has its schedule switched off rather than left firing at somebody's expense.
