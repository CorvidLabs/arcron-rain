# Collecting what you are owed

## Intent

Money is credited and then pulled, never pushed. That is what lets somebody sleep through a drop and collect a week later, and it is what stops one closed wallet from stalling the schedule for everybody else. Collecting should be cheap to attempt, honest about what is waiting, and safe to fail: nothing owed is lost by a try that does not work.

## Criteria

- **CLAIM-1**  Somebody owed a share collects it whenever they like, not at the moment it fell.
- **CLAIM-2**  Nothing is ever pushed at somebody's account, so one closed wallet cannot stall the drops everybody else is waiting for.
- **CLAIM-3**  Somebody can see what a rain owes them before spending a fee to collect it.
- **CLAIM-4**  Asking to collect with nothing owed is harmless rather than an error.
- **CLAIM-5**  Somebody collecting from a gated rain still has to hold a token from the collection at that moment.
  - **CLAIM-5.a**  Somebody who has since sold the token keeps the credit until they hold one again.
- **CLAIM-6**  Somebody collecting a token prize is walked through opting in to hold it before they try to collect.
- **CLAIM-7**  A collection that fails leaves the credit exactly where it was.
- **CLAIM-8**  Somebody collecting a token prize reads the amount in whole tokens rather than raw base units.
- **CLAIM-9**  Collecting is one signature that already carries the fee the payout needs.
- **CLAIM-10**  Somebody who checked in for a drop has that share banked by their next action on the rain, up until the following drop reclaims it.
- **CLAIM-11**  A rain pays each share exactly once, however often somebody asks for it.
