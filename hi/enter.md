# Getting in

## Intent

For somebody holding the right NFT, getting into a rain should be one action, once, and then nothing at all. A ticket is standing rather than a subscription to renew — except on the shape where turning up is the whole point, where the page has to say that instead of promising drops that will never arrive. The gate should read as a collection a person recognises, not as a list of asset ids. Its honest limits belong in front of whoever is choosing it rather than in front of whoever is caught by them.

## Criteria

- **ENTER-1**  Somebody who qualifies takes a ticket once and is in every drop after that.
  - **ENTER-1.a**  On a rain that falls on whoever showed up, a ticket only buys the right to check in.
  - **ENTER-1.b**  Somebody entering that shape is told what a ticket buys before they sign, rather than promised every drop.
- **ENTER-2**  One account holds one ticket on a rain, however many times it tries.
- **ENTER-3**  Somebody entering is told before they sign that a ticket costs a little ALGO even when the prize is a token.
- **ENTER-4**  Somebody entering is told that what a ticket costs stays with the hub for good.
- **ENTER-5**  Somebody entering is told there is no way to leave a rain afterwards.
- **ENTER-6**  A rain can be gated on holding anything a particular collection minted, rather than on one listed asset id.
- **ENTER-7**  Somebody holding a token that collection minted is admitted.
  - **ENTER-7.a**  Somebody holding a lookalike from another minter is turned away.
- **ENTER-8**  A rain with no gate lets anybody in.
- **ENTER-9**  Somebody looking at a gated rain can see whether the wallet they connected qualifies, before they try to enter.
- **ENTER-10**  Somebody who does not qualify is told what they would have to hold.
- **ENTER-11**  The token a rain pays out can never be the token that gets somebody in.
- **ENTER-12**  Checking in is one action.
- **ENTER-13**  Checking in is asked only of people on a rain that falls on whoever showed up.
  - **ENTER-13.a**  Somebody checking in once this drop's seats are taken is told they were too late for it.
- **ENTER-14**  Missing one drop never costs somebody their place in the rain.
- **ENTER-15**  Somebody gating a rain is told the gate reads who holds a token at that moment rather than locking anything up.
- **ENTER-16**  Somebody gating a rain is told one NFT can walk through it from as many wallets as somebody cares to fund.
