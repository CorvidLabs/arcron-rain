# arcron-rain

Rain exists so that giving a token away on a schedule stops being a job somebody has to keep doing. Filling a pot should take one sitting and nobody's permission; being in one should take one action and then nothing; and the drops should keep falling whether or not anyone is paying attention. It should feel like weather rather than like a product — quiet, repeatable, and indifferent to who is watching — while being scrupulously honest about the two things weather is not: the pot is finite, and nothing here can be taken back or patched once it is running. Where a promise cannot be backed by evidence, Rain says so in plain words instead of showing a hopeful one.
